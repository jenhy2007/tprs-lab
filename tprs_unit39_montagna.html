Le avventure di Bea: Courmayeur

Italian TPRS Listening Lab • Section 4, Unit 39

👉 Tap Elisa, Bea, La Corda, or Le Foto!

Tap each card to hear the structure in context:

bisogna essere
one needs to be / it is necessary to be
🪝 Hook: Impersonal rule—no subject needed! Just bisogna + infinitive.
🔊
provare a + infinito
to try to do something
🪝 Hook: Always step onto the little stone "a" before trying an action.
🔊
le ha fatte
she took them (the photos)
🪝 Hook: le = feminine plural ("them" for le foto), so fatte ends in -e!
